PrithviWORLD Workbench

A land surface forecasting bench on Harmonized Landsat Sentinel scenes. Frozen Prithvi-EO-2.0-100M-TL encodes each 6.7 km window into a 14×14 token grid. Small dynamics models forecast the next latent state; the frozen Prithvi decoder reconstructs the scene.

  • Pin a study area and index HLS imagery
  • Compare token change and vegetation (NDVI) over time to see the history
  • Train linear, LSTM, S4D, or ConvLSTM forecasters and inspect predicted vs real RGB
